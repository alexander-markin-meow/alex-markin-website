oxo long soup (zuppa lunga)

full-bodied and balanced

  • breweroxo rapid brewer
  • coffee13.5g
  • water140g
  • ratio∼1:10
  • water temperature∼93℃
  • grindpour-over or finer

method

  1. put an aeropress paper filter in the basket and damp it
  2. grind 13.5g of coffee
  3. put the coffee in the basket and tamp
  4. optionally, add a metal or paper filter on top as an extra shower screen
  5. attach the large transparent chamber
  6. put a paper filter in the larger chamber and damp it
  7. pour 140g of water at 93℃
  8. attach the plunger and push slowly

notei keep the paper filter in the transparent chamber and change it once in a while.

aeropress

  • breweraeropress, inverted
  • coffee12g
  • water200g
  • ratio1:16.5
  • water temperature80–83℃
  • grindslightly finer than pour-over

method

  1. set up the aeropress inverted so it stands on the plunger
  2. put a paper filter in the basket and damp it
  3. put the coffee in the aeropress chamber
  4. 0:00pour in 200g as fast as the kettle allows
  5. 0:30stir the grounds slightly and put the filter on
  6. 0:45invert the aeropress and place it on a cup or server
  7. 1:15start pushing slowly
  8. 2:00finish the push, avoiding the hiss at the end

notei recommend against using a metal filter alone; it produces a lot of mud and fogginess in the cup.
