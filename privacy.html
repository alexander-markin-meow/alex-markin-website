visiting the site

github pages hosts the site and logs your ip address for security. this is needed to deliver and protect the site (legitimate interest). github explains its retention and international transfers.

your browser remembers your site appearance for this session and your analytics choice for 180 days. these settings stay on your device.

optional analytics

only if you allow it, google analytics counts page visits using the page address, browser and device details, your ip address, approximate location and a visitor identifier. google receives this data. the legal basis is your consent. advertising features are disabled.

google sets _ga and _ga_* cookies to recognize visitors and sessions for up to six months. raw analytics data may be held for up to 14 months in a standard google analytics property. google may process it outside the eea; see its data protection information and transfer safeguards.

use “turn off analytics” on any page to stop future tracking and delete its cookies. you can also email me about data already collected.
