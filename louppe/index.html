louppe

a free, open-source, keyboard-first photo & video culler for macOS.

point it at a folder, rate keepers with one key, export or clean up — without ever touching your originals. reads RAW + JPEG, including Nikon and Fujifilm files.

features

  • keyboard-only workflowkeys
    f = keep, d = cull, arrow keys to move
  • RAW + JPEG pairingfiles
    filter EXIF by camera, lens, aperture, shutter speed, and ISO
  • safe exports and cleanupsafety
    export keepers to a new folder or trash rejects, reversible with ⌘z
  • native and open sourcecode
    built in SwiftUI, free to use, with automatic updates
